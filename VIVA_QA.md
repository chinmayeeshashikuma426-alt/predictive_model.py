# Viva Questions and Answers

1. What is predictive modeling?
Predictive modeling uses historical data and machine-learning algorithms to predict an outcome for new data.

2. What type of learning is used?
Supervised learning, because the dataset contains a known target variable called Result.

3. What is the target variable?
Result, with two classes: Pass and Fail.

4. Why is Student_ID removed?
It is an identifier and normally does not provide useful predictive information.

5. What is a Decision Tree?
A tree-based algorithm that makes predictions by applying a sequence of feature-based decisions.

6. What is Random Forest?
An ensemble algorithm that combines predictions from many decision trees.

7. Why split the data?
Training data teaches the model, while test data measures performance on unseen examples.

8. What is accuracy?
The proportion of all predictions that are correct.

9. What is precision?
Among predicted positive cases, precision measures how many are actually positive.

10. What is recall?
Among actual positive cases, recall measures how many are correctly identified.

11. What is F1-score?
The harmonic mean of precision and recall.

12. What is a confusion matrix?
A table showing correct and incorrect predictions for each class.

13. What is ROC?
A curve showing the trade-off between true-positive rate and false-positive rate across classification thresholds.

14. What is AUC?
Area Under the ROC Curve. A higher value generally indicates better class discrimination.

15. Why use Random Forest?
It combines many decision trees and generally reduces the instability of a single tree.

16. What libraries are used?
Pandas, Matplotlib, and Scikit-learn.

17. What is overfitting?
When a model learns training data too closely and performs poorly on unseen data.

18. How can overfitting be reduced?
Use train/test evaluation, cross-validation, depth limits, regularization, pruning, or ensemble methods.
